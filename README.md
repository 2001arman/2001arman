## What I actually do

**Architecture that survives handover.**
Clean Architecture with Domain-Driven Design and a feature-first structure. The codebase you get back
is one another developer can pick up without a rewrite.

**The parts that are hard to fake.**
Real-time messaging over WebSocket. Voice and video on WebRTC and VoIP. End-to-end encryption on the
Matrix protocol. AI wired into a flow where it replaces manual review instead of sitting in a demo.

**The whole lifecycle.**
Notery went from an idea to a Google Play listing with no hand-off in between: research, UX,
architecture, the build, the launch, and the support after it.
